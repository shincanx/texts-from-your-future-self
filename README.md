One funny text message from your future self — 130 characters, in the future self's own voice: the haircut grows out, the tattoo doesn't, and the guy from the gym is now your accountant.

> It's me, you, from 2041. The haircut grows out. The tattoo doesn't. The guy from the gym is now your accountant. Apologize to him.

`main.py` is deterministic, uses no network and no dependencies. It writes that one line to `entry.txt` in the output directory (`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, else `/output`).

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) bounty, $2 in USDC.
